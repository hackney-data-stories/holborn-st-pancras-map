Open Government Licence v3.0 covers:

- London Borough of Camden, ward election declarations for 7 May 2026 and the

  codes (dataset `5rhh-fxna`).

Full OGL text:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

**CC BY-SA 4.0** covers the transcriptions of the 2022 and 2026 Camden
declarations taken from the Wikipedia articles *2022 Camden London Borough
Council election* and *2026 Camden London Borough Council election*. They are
used only to recover candidate percentages, which Camden does not publish, and
from those the ballot-paper counts and electorates. Every vote count taken from
Wikipedia was checked against Camden's own declaration and all eleven wards
matched. Reuse of those derived figures should credit Wikipedia and carry the
same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

No personal data is published here beyond the names of candidates who stood for
public election, as they appear in the returning officer's own declaration.
